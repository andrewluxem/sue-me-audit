# Legal hooks

What each check usually maps to, so the report can say why a FAIL matters. This is orientation, not legal advice, and it is US-centric by default. Laws change and vary by state and country. When the app serves the EU, UK, or Canada, say so in the scope summary and note that GDPR, UK GDPR, ePrivacy rules, or PIPEDA may add obligations.

Never state a legal conclusion ("this violates X"). Say "this is the kind of issue X is used to pursue" and recommend counsel for anything CRITICAL.

Sources wanted: every row should eventually link a primary source (statute, regulation, or agency guidance). Pull requests that add or correct sources are the most useful contribution to this file.
